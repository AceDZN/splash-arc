## Exact prompts (sent in this order in the same chat)

### 01-first-30s.png
> Generate an image, 16:9 landscape (1920x1080): a real in-game screenshot of a polished stylized 3D browser game called "Splash Arc" (Hebrew title קשת המים), a summer-camp water-balloon war. Look: high-quality toon-shaded 3D like a Unity / Three.js indie game (Fall Guys / Overcooked charm), soft rim light, warm saturated palette, real depth and soft shadows. NOT an edtech worksheet, NOT flat 2D: something a 13-year-old wants to play. Camera: side-on 3/4 view of the shot plane, slightly elevated and angled about 20 degrees toward the fort, so the throwing arc reads as a true parabola while the beach has depth. Scene: level 1, a sunny Israeli summer camp on a Mediterranean beach: pale sand, turquoise sea behind, eucalyptus trees, a red-and-white wooden lifeguard tower in the background, colorful wooden cabins, a striped beach umbrella. At the LEFT: a big wooden Y slingshot on a stand with a magenta water balloon in its pouch, and our cheerful camper kid in an orange camp T-shirt and bucket hat beside it. A 3 m cardboard wall at x = 2 m, a volleyball net high above at 6 m, and a wooden crate target at x = 8 m with a rival kid in a teal shirt laughing behind it. A glowing dotted parabola arc from the slingshot over the wall down to the crate, with a big glowing draggable vertex handle (a pulsing magenta orb with a drag ring and small up/down arrows) at the arc's peak, a thin cyan dashed vertical axis-of-symmetry line through the vertex, and a yellow height bar. A metre grid with numbered tick marks 0 to 10 along the sand. HUD (Hebrew, right-to-left, clean rounded chunky game UI, Rubik font): top center a large equation card showing  y = −0.31(x − 4)² + 5  written left-to-right, with a, p and k as colored tiles (a magenta, p cyan, k yellow), with small chips below it: קדקוד (4, 5) · ציר סימטרייה x = 4. Top right: level name "שלב 1 · מעל החומה" with 3 balloon icons, and a short mission line under it. Top left: "רטובים 0/1" counter plus pause and mute icons. Bottom: a − / + nudge row, a small fine-mode toggle, and a big round orange fire button labeled "שגר!" at the bottom right corner area. Make it look like a real, fun, juicy game screenshot.
